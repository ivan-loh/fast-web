03 choices

privacy choices.

fast has no account system and no server-side data collection, so privacy controls live on your device.

fast-support@eriad.com

delete app data

Open fast, go to Settings, and choose delete all data to remove local fasting history and check-ins from the app. You can also delete the app from your device.

apple health

Apple Health access is optional. To change access, open iOS Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Health, then fast. You can also manage health records in the Apple Health app.

notifications

Reminder preferences can be changed in fast. System notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings under Notifications.

exports

If you export your data from the app, the exported file is handled by the iOS share sheet. Choose carefully where you save or send it.

server data

There is no server account data to access, correct, or delete because fast uses no accounts and no server-side data collection.

contact

For privacy requests or questions, email fast-support@eriad.com.
